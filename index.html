01 · Business challenge

How can purchase behavior guide retention and marketing decisions?

02 · Dataset

3,900 purchase records with 18 fields; review ratings, shipping, discounts, subscriptions and purchase patterns.

03 · Data preparation

Used Pandas to validate records, fill 37 missing review ratings with the median, and engineer age-group and purchase-frequency fields.

04 · Analysis & dashboard

Answered 10 business questions using PostgreSQL CTEs, subqueries and window functions; segmented customers into New, Returning and Loyal.

05 · Key findings

Express-shipping purchases averaged approximately $65 compared with $58 for Standard shipping. This is an association in the sample, not proof that shipping choice causes higher spending.

06 · Recommendations

Explore loyalty incentives for returning shoppers, targeted promotions by segment, and subscription conversion opportunities.

01 · Business challenge

Which products, dates and categories drive pizza revenue?

02 · Dataset

48,620 order-line records representing 21,350 orders across a year.

03 · Data preparation

Used SQL Server date functions, CAST, aggregation and subqueries to prepare and summarize sales measures.

04 · Analysis & dashboard

Calculated revenue, order volume, pizzas sold and average order value; built two Power BI pages with DAX and date/category filters.

05 · Key findings

Revenue: $817.86K; orders: 21,350; pizzas sold: 49,574; average order value: $38.31. Friday had the most orders, July was the peak month, and Classic led category revenue.

06 · Recommendations

Plan staffing and stock around peak periods, protect availability of top-selling items, and test promotions for low performers.
